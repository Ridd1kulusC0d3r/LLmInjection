# Community Research & Corpora

Community repositories are valuable discovery sources, but they are not automatically authoritative threat intelligence.

LLMInjection catalogs them with source grade **D** unless a specific item can be traced to stronger evidence.

## Prompt / jailbreak corpora

### togg53192-cmd/jailbreaks
https://github.com/togg53192-cmd/jailbreaks

A model-oriented collection of jailbreak/system-instruction artifacts across multiple model families. Useful as a research lead and corpus reference.

**LLMInjection policy:** index metadata and research relevance; do not blindly copy prompts or treat claimed model behavior as verified security findings.

### Awesome GPT Super Prompting
https://github.com/0xSojalSec/Awesome_GPT_Super_Prompting

Community collection spanning prompting, jailbreak-related material and prompt-security research.

## Intake rule

When community content yields a useful claim:

1. record the community source as discovery provenance;
2. find the primary paper/advisory/report;
3. promote the claim only after evidence review;
4. preserve disagreements rather than merging them away.
